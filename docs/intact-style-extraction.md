# INTACT design-language extraction

This theme is an original cross-media system inferred from user-provided INTACT
paper-figure and presentation references. It captures visual principles without
reusing their exact scientific layout, equations, wordmark, institutional logos,
or project imagery.

## Paper language

- Large uppercase navy headings and compact gray explanatory subtitles.
- Pale blue shared/world-model enclosures contrasted with pale blush
  task-specific/direct-control enclosures.
- Thick blue-gray rounded boundaries, blue causal arrows, and coral action paths.
- White equation capsules, nested grammar blocks, numbered multi-domain chips,
  and dense math typesetting paired with bold sans-serif labels.
- Repeated separation of shared representation, intent families, forward
  prediction, direct control, and optional recurrent verification.
- Four stable domain accents: orange, purple, blue, and teal.

## Presentation identity

- Near-black navy canvas with a restrained blue/white/coral identity.
- A strong segmented display word, circular intent-to-action orbit, and a sparse
  particle constellation confined to the hero focal area.
- Thin blue/coral editorial rails, large centered thesis statement, generous
  negative space, and a low metric strip separated by fine dividers.
- White primary typography, cool-gray secondary copy, blue world/shared metrics,
  and coral direct-control/performance metrics.

## Cross-media contract

The `paper` variant is the default light technical system. The `web` and `video`
variants switch surfaces and typography to the dark brand system while preserving
semantic roles: blue remains shared/world structure; coral remains intent/action;
teal remains verified goal or rollout. Particles are optional atmospheric assets,
never a background for small text or charts.
